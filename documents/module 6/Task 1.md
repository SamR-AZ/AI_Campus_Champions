| **Risk**                                                    | **Safeguard mechanism**                                                                                           | **Responsible party**                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Unpublished or sensitive data entered into unapproved tools | Data-sensitivity classification taught in the module; signed guideline acknowledgment; posted approved-tool list. | Lab manager                           |
