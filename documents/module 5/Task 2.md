**Which framework is more immediately actionable for my role? (3 sentences)**

NIST is more immediately actionable for a lab manager: its GOVERN and MAP subcategories translate directly into the SOPs, onboarding checklists, and documentation I already maintain. UNESCO supplies what those SOPs are ultimately for - dignity, environment, equity - and obligations NIST omits, such as carbon footprint and ultimate human responsibility. In practice, I draft with NIST and justify with UNESCO.
