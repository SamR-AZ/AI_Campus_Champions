**Task 4: Unit-Level AI Acceptable Use Guideline (Draft)**

**UNIT/TEAM:** Blankinship Lab (soil science research group), University of Arizona **EFFECTIVE DATE:** 15 July 2026

**PURPOSE**

Lab members already run AI-heavy workflows (our Module 3 canvas documents one); this guideline makes that use consistent, disclosed, and safe for our data and publications.

**SCOPE**

All lab personnel - PI, lab manager, postdocs, students, visiting researchers - for all lab research, data handling, writing.

**KEY DEFINITIONS**

AI-generated content: substance produced mainly by AI with minimal human direction. AI-assisted content: human-directed work where AI helped specific steps. Approved AI tool: cleared by the lab manager under university data-use guidance.

**PERMITTED USES**

(1) Background research and literature synthesis, every fact verified against its original source before research use; (2) drafting and debugging analysis code, human-reviewed; (3) editing one's own text; (4) brainstorming hypotheses, designs.

**RESTRICTED USES**

(1) Above-low-sensitivity data (unpublished datasets, manuscripts, proposals) in tools without approved data terms, including our current literature tool (data-sensitivity risk); (2) human-subjects, partner, or export-controlled information (privacy and compliance risk); (3) AI-generated text submitted as one's own (integrity risk); (4) unreviewed AI exclusion of data points (validity risk).

**DISCLOSURE REQUIREMENTS**

Record tool, model, and use in lab notebooks and code headers; disclose in manuscript methods or acknowledgments, naming tool and model.

**REVIEW**

REVIEW DATE: 1 July 2027. OWNER: Lab Manager (role).
